# World

The concept is in `docs/welt.md`.

The world (`src/world.rs`, concept in `docs/welt.md`) consists of the Tiled maps in `assets/world.world`; bevy_ecs_tiled only spawns those that the area of `TiledWorldChunking` around the camera touches and despawns the others with all their children. `world::update_chunking` keeps that area at least as large as the view and `world::MIN_LOADED_DISTANCE` (what the flow field and the sight need around the player). Each loaded map gets a `MapArea`; the flow field, the sight and the sun's shadows keep a raster of the map's colliders on the map's entity (`world::MapRaster`, built by `world::rasterize` when the map is loaded and again only when one of its colliders changes; `MapRaster::Data` reads more of each collider, like its height), so it disappears with the map. Systems putting the rasters of the loaded maps together take `world::MapRasters`, which tells when a map was loaded, rasterized again or unloaded. Both work on one raster of 4 px cells over the whole world (`src/cells.rs`), on which all maps lie since they are placed in whole tiles. Enemies have no parent; `enemy::despawn_outside_maps` removes those that stand on no loaded map (with `UNLOAD_MARGIN`) once a second. Items stay when their map is unloaded.
