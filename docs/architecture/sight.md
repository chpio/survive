# Sight

The concept is in `docs/sicht.md`.

Sight (`src/sight/`, concept in `docs/sicht.md`): colliders with `BlocksSight` are rasterized into each map's `sight::OpaqueCells`. Around the player's cell, `Sight` keeps a mask of how visible each cell is (in the player's `sight::SightRadius`, fading out, nothing behind opaque cells; computed by recursive shadowcasting, since letting each cell inherit from its neighbor towards the player snaps shadow edges to 45° steps), in a square window sized for `MAX_SIGHT_RADIUS` that moves with the player. Which cells are lit is only computed again when the player changes cells or the loaded maps change; when only the radius changes (every frame during the twilight), the mask is put together again from them. `SightRadius` is a required component of `Player` with a base value (set by the day-night cycle) and a final one (capped; later with bonuses from equipment). The window's opaque cells are put together from the loaded maps then; outside of them, nothing blocks the sight. `sight::render` writes it into a linearly filtered texture (`render::mask_image`, also used for the light's masks) that `SightMaterial` (shader `assets/shaders/sight.wesl`) samples once at each pixel's world position; the mask is blurred over `render::BLUR` on the CPU before (`render::blur`, a fixed kernel per cell, since 16 samples per pixel cost about 1.8 ms per layer at 4K): enemies use it so only their parts in sight are drawn, and a large dark rectangle above the map, below items and units, uses it inverted to darken the map outside the sight. Outside the sight only the map is drawn: enemies and items on the ground are masked by `SightMaterial`, and health bars, which are sprites and can't be masked, carry `sight::render::HiddenOutsideSight` and are hidden as a whole while `Sight::shows` doesn't see their position, through a holder in the middle of their unit, so the bar's own visibility only depends on the damage. New `SightMaterial`s start with the mask's current area (kept in `SightMask`). `Sight::sees` tells whether any part of a circle is in sight; sleeping enemies wake up by it, and spawners use it to stay out of sight.
