# Movement

Movement is force-based (`src/movement.rs`). Systems set a `DesiredVelocity` on Avian bodies (player and enemies: `RigidBody::Dynamic` with `Mass` and `Strength`) instead of changing `Transform` or `LinearVelocity` directly. In `FixedUpdate`, a spring towards an anchor moving at that velocity applies a force limited by `Strength`, so heavier and stronger bodies win when pushing each other. After the desired velocity changes, the body first changes its velocity straight to it at full strength (and the anchor starts again at the body), so it neither overshoots nor snaps back when starting or stopping. Avian runs with `movement::LENGTH_UNIT` (16 px, one tile) and `movement::CONTACT_TOLERANCE`, in `main.rs` and `tests/common.rs` alike; with a larger contact tolerance, bodies sliding along the map's triangulated colliders get pushed away at corners. Bodies are built with `movement::body`, have no friction (so they slide along obstacles instead of sticking at corners) and never rotate; an enemy's visible `enemy::Body` is a child showing its figure, which faces its `enemy::Heading` (the direction its navigation wants to walk, unaffected by knockback; `enemy::face_heading` turns `FigureControl::facing` towards it, limited by `TURN_SPEED`). All enemies show the beetle figure (`docs/architecture/figure.md`), scaled to their radius. The camera follows the player's `Transform` and keeps its own z.
